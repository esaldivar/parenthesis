## What's new for families

### Messages (#125)

- **One conversation between the parents.** Messages arrive live, with an unread dot in the navigation, a "New
  messages" divider, typing, and **Read** under your last message once your co-parent has read it. A sent message
  can't be edited or deleted. If a message fails to send it stays as **Not sent** with Retry, which never sends it
  twice. Dictation is your phone keyboard's own microphone; Send stays a separate step.
- **Share a calendar event.** Search your calendar by name or date and send the event as a card. Each parent's card
  follows their own calendar: a proposal says it's pending, the other parent's unavailable time shows only as
  "Unavailable", and a cancelled event shows as no longer available.
- **Compact chat on desktop.** Minimize docks the same conversation over the page you came from, with the same draft.
- **PDF transcripts.** Choose all messages or a date range (both dates included, in the family's time zone), preview
  exactly what the PDF holds, then **Download PDF** to your device or **Save transcript** to keep an unchanging copy
  in Parenthesis. Saved transcripts are yours alone. On a phone, the document icon in the chat header opens them.

### Child Essentials (#126)

- **Pick a child, then their essentials.** Either parent can edit the shared details directly. Every section keeps
  its history (who changed what, when, and the values before and after). If both parents edit at once, the second
  parent's draft is kept and the latest saved values are shown to review before saving again.
- **Sections:** School (grade, homeroom teacher through fifth grade, parent concerns); Allergies & Food (allergies
  first, or **No known allergies** with who recorded it); Clothing & Shoes; What Helps; and Routines.
- **Allergy PDF.** A printable PDF for one child or all children, dated when made.
- **Routines** (#126): Shared routines for both homes, plus **My routines** and your co-parent's routines. Your
  co-parent can choose **Use in both homes**; either parent can **Stop sharing**.
- **Emergency Contacts** (#129): one **Call first** contact pinned at the top, tap-to-call numbers, and copying a
  contact to another child.
- **Shared Caregivers** (#130): name, number, town and rate, **School pickup**, and each parent's own one-to-three-star
  rating, averaged.
- **Updates from your co-parent** (#128). When your co-parent changes a shared detail, Child Essentials gets a dot
  (under More on phones). Opening it shows a banner such as "Kai’s shoe size updated" (never the new value), and the
  child, the section and the changed row stay marked until you've seen them. Each mark clears on its own, and the
  change stays in history. On Routines, your co-parent's tab gets a dot until you open it. Star ratings, "Use in both
  homes" and "Stop sharing" make no update.

Children never see the parents' messages or Child Essentials.

## Database changes

Eleven new migrations, applied automatically on deployment:

- Messaging: messages and read positions, shared calendar events on messages, saved transcripts.
- Families: revisions for Clothing & Shoes, What Helps, School, Allergies & Food and Routines; emergency contacts;
  shared caregivers; and Child Essentials update notices.

## Behind the scenes

- The API's first live connection: a listen-only SignalR hub at `/hubs/chat` for parents, with membership and
  same-site checks ([ADR-0008](../adr/0008-parent-messaging.md)).
- PDFs are drawn in the API with SkiaSharp and embedded Noto Sans (SIL Open Font License). Transcript text covers
  Latin, Greek and Cyrillic; other alphabets are tracked in `TODO.md`.
- Browser tests for chat, event cards, transcripts and Child Essentials updates join the Release E2E gate.
