## What's new for families

- **The shared calendar.** Week (the default), Month and List, in the family's time zone. Each event shows its child,
  its parents and its status in words, never colour alone. Pending items are hatched and labelled. Unavailable time
  is grey and shows only "Unavailable" to the other parent. There's a key on larger screens (#156).
- **Add to calendar.** Activities, parenting time, family events and your own unavailable time. Parenting time and
  family events wait for the co-parent's approval and are never shown as confirmed until then (#156).
- **Approvals for calendar changes.** Requests appear in the Approvals inbox with what changes (before → after),
  plus Approve, Decline with a comment, and Withdraw. Gentle warnings appear when dates overlap the other parent's
  unavailable time; they never block a request (#156, #159). The parent who decides can also Approve or Decline
  right from the event's details on the calendar (#218).
- **No parenting time while you're unavailable.** A parent can't have the children and be marked unavailable at the
  same time: the app refuses either overlap (pending parenting time counts) and says so, naming the parent, never the
  private note. Overlaps from before stay as they are (#210).
- **Request a parenting-time swap.** Pick new dates with an optional reason (shown in the app only, never by email).
  Review it, send it, and follow it until it's decided (#159).
- **Edit an event.** Title, time, place, notes and the caregiver. Changes to parenting time or family events go to the
  co-parent for approval as "Change pending"; other changes apply at once (#188).
- **Repeating activities and unavailable time.** Choose the days of the week, ending on a date or after a number of
  times, within a year. A preview shows the dates. Change or cancel This event, This and following, or All events
  (#193).
- **Declined requests.** A declined new event stays on both parents' calendars as **Declined** (struck through), with
  who declined it, when and why. Each parent can remove it from their own calendar; it also drops off once its dates
  pass (#199).
- **Reminders.** Shared notes for a day, for both parents, which can repeat daily, weekly or monthly. They show who
  edited them and whether the other parent has seen them (#72).
- **Shared tasks.** To-dos due on a day, with Mark done, optional checklists (required and optional items) that
  complete themselves, and a small celebration (#73).
- **Calendar filters.** Filter by type, parent and approval status. Your choices are remembered in your browser and
  are personal to you. Hidden unavailable time stays findable as a subtle marker, with a swap option when it
  overlaps that parent's parenting time (#190).
- **The home page uses real data.** The week, Upcoming (requests waiting for you, then events, reminders and tasks)
  and Quick Add's Event and Note. Messages and memories still say they aren't available yet (#196).

Children see only confirmed events that include them: never reminders, tasks, unavailable time, or anything pending
or declined.
