<h1 align="center">Parenthesis</h1>

<p align="center"><strong>Different homes. Same love.</strong></p>

Parenthesis helps separated parents coordinate family life around their children. Keep a shared schedule,
organize everyday tasks, and agree on parenting-time changes with a clear view of what is confirmed and
what still needs a decision. Both parents have an equal say.

## More clarity for both homes

- **One shared calendar.** See activities, parenting time and family events by week, month or list,
  in your family's time zone.
- **Changes you can agree on.** Propose parenting time or a swap, review the details, and approve or decline.
  Pending requests stay clearly separate from confirmed plans.
- **Everyday things, together.** Share reminders and tasks, with repeating reminders and optional checklists.
- **A useful view of the week.** Find upcoming plans and filter the calendar by child, parent, event type
  or approval status.

These capabilities are part of the calendar work currently being tested. Parenthesis is still in development;
they are not a promise of a finished public service.

## Built around your family

Parents have equal authority, and each family's information belongs within that family. Children have a
separate experience that keeps adult approvals and disagreements out of their view. Light, dark and system
appearance settings are personal to each user.

Messaging, shared memories, artwork and a private child diary are planned for later milestones. See the
[roadmap](docs/planning/mvp-backlog.md#release-map-owner-decision-october-18-2026) for scope and sequencing.
