# Child Essentials section illustrations (#126)

Cut on November 7, 2026 from the owner's Child Essentials desktop mockup (light theme), at the owner's request in the
Claude Code session: "Cut from your mockups". Each watercolor was cropped, upscaled 3x, and its tile background removed
so it sits on the light and dark section tints. No logo, people's faces, names or photographs.

These are stand-ins taken from a screenshot, so they are softer than original art files. Replace them with the original
illustration exports, at about 400px wide, when they're available (tracked in TODO.md). Files: `school`, `allergies`,
`routines`, `clothing`, `what-helps`, `caregivers`, `emergency` (`.webp`, transparent).

`medical.webp` (#501) uses the approved standalone watercolor bag and stethoscope from the Medical mockup. It was cropped to its transparent bounds and exported for the section tile and header.

`what-helps-cutout.webp` (#667) is the cleaned bunny raster embedded in the approved What Helps Figma frames (page
`878:194`, layer "Centered original What Helps artwork behind translucent content"), downloaded from Figma on November
18, 2026 and saved as WebP with its transparency intact: the gaps between the ears, arms, body and legs are fully
transparent. It sits behind the What Helps section; the tile keeps `what-helps.webp`.

`caregivers-landscape.webp` and `caregivers-portrait.webp` (#664) are the exact uploaded artworks embedded in the
approved Shared Caregivers Figma frames (page `626:194`): the full landscape behind the desktop content and the
caregiver reading with two children behind the mobile content. Downloaded from Figma on November 18, 2026 and saved as
WebP without editing; the app mirrors the portrait horizontally, as the frames do.

`emergency-telephone.webp` (#665) is the emergency telephone artwork embedded in the approved Emergency Contacts Figma
frames (page `715:194`, layer "Centered original emergency artwork behind translucent content"), downloaded from Figma
on November 18, 2026 at its embedded size (224x145) and saved losslessly as WebP.

`tiles/*.webp` (#669) are the eight cleaned, high-resolution, full-color category illustrations embedded in the approved
Child Essentials landing-page Figma frames (`1321:2`, `1321:56`, `1321:110`, `1321:168`), downloaded from Figma on
November 18, 2026. All four frames embed byte-identical files. Each was scaled (premultiplied, so the edges gain no
fringe) to fit 336x240, three times the largest card slot (112x80), and saved losslessly as WebP with its transparency,
uncropped so it sits in the card exactly as in the frames. Shared Caregivers is the approved caregiver reading with two
children, not the old hands or line art. They are used on the landing cards only; the section screens keep their own
artwork.
