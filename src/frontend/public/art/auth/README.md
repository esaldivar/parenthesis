# Sign-in background art (#697)

Botanical background for the sign-in, sign-up, profile picker and PIN pages on wider screens, light and dark.
Exported unchanged at 1280 × 800 from the approved Figma designs (Parenthesis Design System, page 17 · Account ·
Login & Sign Up, "REQUEST #1 · 10 Desktop" background) on November 21, 2026, then converted to WebP. The art was
generated in Figma with its AI image tool for the owner's Request #1 sheet; it has no text, logo, people or
photographs.

The pages use it only from 768 px wide, behind the centred card. It's decorative: forced colours and printing
omit it, and the page reads the same without it.
